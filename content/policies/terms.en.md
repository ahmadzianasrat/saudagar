# Saudagar — Terms, Pricing & Policies

## Subscription Plans

- **Free Trial** — 30 days, full access to the ledger and inventory features, no payment required. Starts automatically when your account is approved.
- **Monthly** — 250 AFN per month.
- **6 Months** — 1,250 AFN, which saves you approximately 17% compared to paying monthly.

## What happens if your subscription expires

You can always view your existing ledger and inventory records, with no time limit. However, adding new ledger entries or inventory transactions requires an active subscription. Renew any time to resume adding new entries — nothing you've already recorded is ever hidden or deleted due to an expired subscription.

## Refunds & Cancellation

Subscription payments are not refundable, including partial refunds for unused time. If you cancel or do not renew, your access to add new entries continues until the end of the period you already paid for, after which the account moves to read-only as described above.

## Record-Keeping & Disputes

Saudagar is a record-keeping tool. Ledger entries reflect what each shop owner records and are not independently verified by Saudagar. We do not mediate or resolve disagreements between trading partners about a transaction's amount or terms. If you and a trading partner disagree about an entry, we encourage you to confirm the transaction directly with them — sharing your WhatsApp balance summary can help settle most disagreements quickly.

## Prices Shown in the App

Market prices shown in Saudagar are provided for reference and are uploaded by market representatives for the markets covered by the app. Saudagar does not guarantee these prices exactly match every transaction available in a given market at a given moment, and you should confirm current terms directly with a buyer or seller before relying on a price shown in the app for a specific trade.
